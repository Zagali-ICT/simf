## Creating an account

1. Open the app or the website and choose to register.
2. Enter your **email address** and a **password**, and confirm the password.
   The password must be at least 8 characters and contain at least one letter
   and one digit.
3. SIMF sends a **six-digit verification code** to your email address. The code
   is valid for ten minutes.
4. Enter the code to verify your email address. If the code has expired,
   request a new one — requesting a new code cancels the previous one.

After your email is verified you continue with your registration details. A
visitor account is then reviewed and approved before it can be used.
