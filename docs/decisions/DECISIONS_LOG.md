| D-060 | 2026-05-25 | **H5 — `OnTokenValidated` requires a non-empty `security_stamp` claim and compares it in constant time.** `JwtBearerSetup.OnTokenValidatedAsync` now does three checks instead of one: (a) the `security_stamp` claim must be present in the JWT and non-empty (otherwise audit `AccessTokenRejected` with detail `"security stamp claim missing or empty"` and 401); (b) the DB row's `SecurityStamp` must also be non-empty (defensive — no token validates against a null/empty DB stamp); (c) the comparison runs through a new `FixedTimeEquals` helper that converts both sides to UTF-8 bytes, checks length equality, and calls `CryptographicOperations.FixedTimeEquals`.
