This is the official application of the 4th Saudi International Maritime Forum, a
government-sponsored maritime conference held at the Sofitel Riyadh Hotel and

Apps for earlier editions of this forum

The forum has been held before, and earlier editions were served by their own
apps, published by whichever technical vendor the organizer had contracted at the
time. Those authorizations covered those editions only. If your review has
compared this submission with one of them, that is the likely reason, and the
answer is the attached letter: it is dated 1 September 2026, it is issued by the
same event organizer, and it names Zagali Multi Activities Limited as the
technical partner for the 4th edition. We have no relationship with the
publishers of the earlier apps and no control over their listings. Startime
Events Company, as the licensed organizer, can confirm directly to Apple that any
earlier listing is not the official app of the 4th edition, and can request its
removal.
