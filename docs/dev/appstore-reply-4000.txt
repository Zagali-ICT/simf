1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached, captured on a physical iPhone on the current iOS. From launch it shows guest browsing, sign-in, the programme, seat reservation, the badge QR, a question, contact exchange, registration with the identity step, and deletion.
Deletion is fully in-app: Profile > My Area > "Delete my account" > confirm. Immediate and irreversible, with no support ticket. It erases the profile data, the identity document, the ID image and face photograph, every session and device, and the sign-in address. It is available to accounts awaiting organiser approval as well as approved ones.
User content: no public feed, chat, comment wall or public Q&A. Questions sent to a session reach only a staff moderator and are never shown to other attendees. The only attendee-written content another attendee sees is a meeting request's subject line, read by the invited counterpart alone. Every account is identity-verified with a government ID and a live face capture and approved by the organiser, so no attendee is anonymous and the organiser can disable any account.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
The official companion app for the Saudi International Maritime Forum, a public maritime event in Saudi Arabia, for the general public attending it. It carries the programme, seat booking, a scannable admission badge, the venue and exhibition maps, and contact exchange, in Arabic and English. A few staff screens (gate scanning, a moderator desk) are assigned by an administrator and cannot be self-selected; the app is otherwise entirely a public attendee product, which is why we seek public distribution.
