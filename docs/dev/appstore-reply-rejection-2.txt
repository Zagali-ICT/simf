Thank you for the review. Both guidelines are addressed below.

Guideline 5.1.1(v), account deletion

Deletion is entirely in the app and needs no email or support request.

The "Delete my account" button now appears on every screen that can hold an
account, including the registration form and the approval-status screen. That is
the fix: previously it was only in My Area, which an account awaiting organiser
approval never reaches, so a reviewer stopped at the registration step could not
find it.

To test it: sign in with the demo account, tap "Delete my account", confirm, then
enter the six-digit code we email to the account's own address. The demo mailbox
login is in App Review Information so you can read that code. Deleting erases the
profile, the identity document and the photographs, revokes every session and
device, and cannot be undone.
